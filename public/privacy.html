Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Buzzword Bingo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The app runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sent to any server, and the app makes no network requests.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published with the app.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pag3/buzzword-bingo.
